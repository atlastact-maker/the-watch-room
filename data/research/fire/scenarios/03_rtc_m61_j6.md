## Static facts

- **Location:** M61 northbound, approx 1.8 miles south of J6 (Horwich Parkway), in lanes 1 and 2
- **Coords:** approx 53.566, -2.521
- **Type:** multi-vehicle RTC — HGV vs two cars
- **Occupants:** HGV driver (out of cab), Car 1 (couple, 60s — entrapment), Car 2 (single male, 25 — walking wounded)
- **Hazards:** HGV diesel tank ruptured, fluids on carriageway, traffic flow continuing in lane 3 until closure imposed
- **Access:** motorway only — closure required; nearest exit J5 (Westhoughton) southbound or J6 (Horwich) northbound
- **First-due station:** G52 Horwich (1× WrL, day-crewed)
- **Required specials:** TRU R57 from G57 Leigh (Technical Response Pump — extrication kit)

## PRI (synthetic)

- N/A (open road).

## Escalation triggers

- "Fire from HGV" → **make pumps 4**, foam pod (BFU from G53 Farnworth), HVP if vehicle fully involved.
- "Secondary collision in queue" → multi-incident management, additional resources from G50 / G54.
- "Fatality confirmed" → DI (police), HM coroner attendance, extended cordon.

## Game evaluation

Targets:
- Time to scene: **< 12 minutes** (motorway target)
- Highways closure request issued in **first METHANE** (or first 2 minutes after mobilisation)
- Casualty extrication: **< 30 minutes** (golden hour preserved)

Penalties:
- Fail to request closure → secondary collision risk roll
- Send pump only (no rescue pump) → extrication takes much longer
- ALP not pre-positioned → if HGV fire develops, no aerial coverage
